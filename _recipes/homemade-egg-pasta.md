directions:
- Combine flour and salt in a food processor.
- Add eggs and water to the flour mixture and process until a dough forms.
- Knead the dough for 8 minutes using a fold-and-press technique.
- Wrap the dough in plastic wrap and let it rest for at least 30 minutes.
- Divide the dough into four parts and roll out each piece using a pasta machine, starting from the widest setting and gradually decreasing the thickness.
- Let the pasta sheets dry for 15-30 minutes before cutting into ribbons.
- Cook the fresh pasta in heavily salted water for 1-2 minutes.
- Transfer the cooked pasta directly to the sauce without draining.

components:
- 300 g flour (preferably double zero flour)
- 2 large eggs
- 3 large egg yolks
- Water (to reach a total of 185 g with the eggs)
- 2 teaspoons Diamond Crystal kosher salt (or 1 teaspoon table salt)

---

Create your own fresh egg pasta at home with this simple recipe. Using a food processor and pasta machine, you can achieve perfect homemade pasta every time. Enjoy the rich flavor and texture of fresh pasta in your favorite dishes.
