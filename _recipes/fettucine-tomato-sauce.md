ingredients:
- 75 ml olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 kg cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/2 teaspoon caster sugar (adjust based on tomato sweetness)
- 1 dried ancho chili, torn apart
- 20 g basil leaves
- 400 g fettuccine (or spaghetti)
- 35 g Parmesan, finely grated
- Salt

directions:
- Heat olive oil in a large sauté pan over medium-high heat. Add garlic and fry until caramelized.
- Add tomatoes, sugar, chili, and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Pour in 200 ml water and stir until tomatoes - rt to break down.
- Reduce heat to medium-low and cook for about 1 hour, stirring occasionally, until the sauce - ckens. Stir in basil and keep warm.
- Boil salted water in a large saucepan, cook pasta until al dente, and drain.
- Toss pasta with the tomato sauce, divide into bowls, sprinkle with Parmesan, and serve.

---

This fettuccine dish features a rich, smoky tomato sauce made with ripe cherry tomatoes and ancho chili. It's perfect for a flavorful and comforting meal, and the sauce can be made in larger batches to store for later use.
