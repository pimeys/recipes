directions:
- Preheat oven to 220°C with a combination steam setting.
- Mix half the onion, minced meat, breadcrumbs, cheese, and egg. Form into 24 meatballs and place on a pan.
- Cook meatballs until browned, about 12 minutes.
- Mix remaining onion and garlic, add to pan, and cook until softened.
- Reduce oven to 160°C, add tomato passata and water, and cook for 30 minutes.
- Serve with spaghetti, topped with parmesan and basil.

components:
- 1 large onion, finely chopped and divided
- 500 g minced meat (pork and veal mix, or one type)
- 75 g fresh breadcrumbs (or 1/2 cup dried breadcrumbs with water)
- 75 g parmesan cheese, finely grated
- 1 egg, lightly beaten
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 750 ml tomato passata (or 700 ml crushed tomatoes)
- 250 ml water

---

Delicious oven-baked meatballs in a rich tomato sauce, perfect for serving over spaghetti. This recipe offers both steam and conventional oven methods for versatile cooking.
