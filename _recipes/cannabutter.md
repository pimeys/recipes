components:
- Mesh strainer or cheesecloth
- Container for cannabutter
- 227 g unsalted butter (2 sticks)
- 240 ml water
- 7-10 g ground cannabis, decarboxylated

directions:
  - Preheat the oven to 120°C.
  - Spread the loosely broken cannabis on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  - Heat for 30-40 minutes, gently mixing every 10-15 minutes.
  - Grind or break up the cannabis to a consistent texture, avoiding a fine powder.
  - Melt the butter in a saucepan over low heat.
  - Add water to regulate temperature and prevent scorching.
  - Add the decarboxylated cannabis to the melted butter.
  - Simmer on low heat for 2-3 hours, stirring occasionally.
  - Pour the mixture through a mesh strainer or cheesecloth into a container.
  - Discard the plant material without squeezing out excess butter to avoid adding chlorophyll.
  - Place the jar in the refrigerator and let it solidify, ideally overnight.
  - If excess water forms, remove the solid butter and drain the water.

---

This recipe provides a simple method for making cannabutter, which can be used as a base for various edibles. Enjoy responsibly and ensure you follow local laws and regulations regarding cannabis use.
