directions:
- Sauté guanciale in olive oil until edges are lightly browned. Set aside and keep warm.
- Boil 5 liters of water with 3 tablespoons of salt, then cook pasta until al dente.
- Whisk eggs, cheese, and pepper in a bowl until smooth.
- Drain pasta, reserving some water, and toss with guanciale over low heat.
- Pour the egg mixture into the pasta, tossing quickly to coat. Add tiny bit of the pasta water to prevent the sauce from curdling. Use a warm spot to keep the mixture creamy.
- Serve immediately in heated bowls.

components:
- 115 g guanciale, diced
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 3 large eggs, at room temperature
- 100 g freshly grated pecorino romano (or half pecorino romano and half parmigiano-reggiano)
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 450 g pasta (spaghetti, bucatini, penne, or rigatoni)


---

This classic Italian carbonara features a creamy sauce made from eggs, cheese, and guanciale, perfectly coating the pasta. Enjoy it with your choice of pasta shape and a glass of your favorite wine.
