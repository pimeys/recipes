directions:
- Preheat the oven to 135°C. Season the chuck roast with salt and pepper, and tie with twine.
- Brown the beef in a Dutch oven, then set aside.
- Mix anchovies, garlic, Marmite, soy sauce, and tomato paste into a paste.
- Cook carrots, celery, and onions until softened, then add the anchovy paste and flour.
- Deglaze with wine, reduce, then add chicken stock with gelatin, bay leaves, and thyme.
- Simmer beef in the oven for 3-4 hours, adding potatoes 45 minutes before it's done.
- Cool, refrigerate overnight, then remove fat and reheat the meat.
- Slice the beef, reheat with sauce, and serve with vegetables and gravy.

components:
- 2.27 kg boneless chuck roast, trimmed and tied
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 30 ml vegetable oil
- 4 anchovy fillets
- 2 medium cloves garlic, finely minced (about 2 teaspoons)
- 1 teaspoon Marmite
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 large carrots, peeled and cut into chunks
- 2 stalks celery, cut into chunks
- 2 large onions, finely sliced (about 4 cups)
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 750 ml dry red wine
- 960 ml homemade or low-sodium canned chicken stock
- 1 packet (7 g) unflavored gelatin
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 sprigs fresh thyme
- 454 g russet baking potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks

---

A classic pot roast recipe with tender beef, rich gravy, and hearty vegetables. Perfect for a comforting family meal, this dish is slow-cooked to perfection.
