title:  "Roasted Aubergine with Anchovies and Oregano"
image: roasted-aubergine-anchovies.jpg

tags:
- side dish
- vegetarian

directions:
- Preheat the oven to 220°C fan.
- Mix aubergine slices with salt, place on baking trays, brush with oil, and bake for 35 minutes until golden-brown.
- Whisk together anchovies, vinegar, garlic, salt, and pepper. Slowly add remaining oil while whisking.
- Chop oregano and combine with aubergine and parsley. Pour the anchovy dressing over and mix gently before serving.

components:
- 4 medium aubergines, sliced into 2 cm thick discs (1 kg)
- 100 ml olive oil
- 20g anchovy fillets in oil, drained and finely chopped
- 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 1 small garlic clove, crushed
- 1 tbsp picked oregano leaves
- 5g parsley leaves, roughly chopped
- Salt and black pepper

---

A delightful side dish that pairs roasted aubergine with a savory anchovy dressing. Perfect for serving with toasted sourdough or as a complement to roast chicken or fresh tuna.
