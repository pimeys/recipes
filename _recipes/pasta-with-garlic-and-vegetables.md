directions:
- Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add 225g of asparagus and cook until bright green and tender-crisp, about 3 minutes. Using a fine-mesh strainer, transfer to a colander and run under cold water to chill. Repeat with the zucchini, summer squash, fava beans, and broccoli, one vegetable at a time. If using fava beans, peel them. Combine all the blanched vegetables in a large bowl, add the peas and cherry tomatoes, and set aside.
- Heat 60ml of olive oil and 4 smashed garlic cloves in a 25cm skillet over medium-high heat until the garlic is gently sizzling. Reduce the heat to medium-low and sizzle until the garlic is golden brown, 5 to 7 minutes. Discard the garlic cloves, leaving the oil in the skillet, and return the pan to high heat. Add the sliced garlic and cook, stirring, until garlic just begins to turn pale golden brown, about 45 seconds. Add minced garlic and pepper flakes and toss until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Remove from the heat and stir in 30g of butter. Set aside.
- Place 450g of short twisted or tubular pasta in a large pot and cover with hot water. Add a pinch of salt and bring to a boil over high heat, stirring occasionally to keep the pasta from sticking. Continue to cook until the pasta is softened but retains a slight bite in the center (about 1 minute less than the box recommends after the pasta reaches a boil). Drain the pasta, reserving 120ml of the cooking liquid, and return to the pot over medium-low heat.
- Add the vegetables, garlic sauce, and half of the reserved pasta water and stir until the sauce comes together and coats the pasta, adding more pasta water as necessary until the desired consistency is reached. Stir in 15g of chopped fresh parsley, 1 tsp of grated lemon zest, 15ml of lemon juice, and 28g of grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, and season to taste with salt and pepper. Serve immediately, passing additional Parmesan at the table.

ingredients:
- Kosher salt
- 225g asparagus, ends trimmed and cut on a bias into 5cm segments
- 1 medium zucchini, split lengthwise and cut into 6mm half-moons
- 1 medium summer squash, split lengthwise and cut into 3mm half-moons
- 225g shelled fava beans (optional)
- 225g broccoli florets cut into 1.25cm pieces (optional)
- 225g frozen peas, thawed
- 225g grape or cherry tomatoes, split in half (optional)
- 60ml extra-virgin olive oil
- 12 medium cloves garlic, 4 smashed and left whole, 4 finely sliced, 4 minced or grated on a Microplane
- Pinch of red pepper flakes
- 30g unsalted butter
- 450g short twisted or tubular pasta such as gemelli, cavatappi, or rotini
- 15g chopped fresh parsley
- 1 tsp grated lemon zest and 15ml juice (from 1 lemon)
- 28g Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated, plus more for serving
- Freshly ground black pepper

---

Enjoy this delicious and healthy pasta dish, perfect for spring with its abundance of fresh vegetables.
