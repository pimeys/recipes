tags:

directions:
- Toast dried chiles in a Dutch oven until darkened and aromatic.
- Simmer chiles in chicken stock until softened.
- Blend the mixture until smooth, adding water if needed.
- Freeze in ice cube trays for easy portioning and storage.

components:
- 6 ancho, pasilla, or mulato chiles (about 14 g), seeded and torn
- 5 New Mexico red, California, costeño, or choricero chiles (about 14 g), seeded and torn
- 2 cascabel, árbol, or pequin chiles, seeded and torn
- 480 ml homemade or low-sodium canned chicken stock

---

A versatile chile paste that can be used in place of chili powder in any recipe. Freeze in portions for convenient use throughout the year.
