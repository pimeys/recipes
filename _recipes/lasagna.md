directions:
- Soak the lasagna noodles in warm water for about 15 minutes until lightly softened.
- Melt the butter in a saucepan, add flour, and whisk until lightly golden. Add garlic, then whisk in milk until smooth. Add cheese and nutmeg, and melt. Season with salt and pepper.
- Drain and dry the noodles.
- Preheat the oven to 190°C. Layer Bolognese sauce, béchamel, cheese, and noodles in a baking dish.
- Bake for about 45 minutes until golden and bubbly. Let cool for 10 minutes, then sprinkle with herbs before serving.

components:
- 2 recipes of homemade egg pasta
- 28 g unsalted butter
- 28 g all-purpose flour
- 2 medium cloves garlic, finely minced or grated (about 2 teaspoons)
- 480 ml whole milk
- 225 g whole-milk mozzarella cheese, grated
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly grated nutmeg
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1 recipe Basic Bolognese Sauce, warm
- 113 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated (about 2 cups)
- 2 tablespoons minced fresh basil or parsley (or a mix)

---

A classic Italian dish, this lasagna features layers of pasta, rich Bolognese sauce, and creamy béchamel. Perfect for feeding a crowd or enjoying as comfort food.
