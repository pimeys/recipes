---

title:  "15-Minute Pantry Tomato Soup"
image: tomato-soup.jpg

tags:
- soup
- quick
- vegetarian

components:
- 42 g unsalted butter
- 1 large onion, finely diced (about 225 g)
- Pinch of red pepper flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- Two 800 g cans whole tomatoes, with juice
- 120 ml whole milk or heavy cream
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 30 ml whiskey, vodka, or brandy (optional)
- 30 ml extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh herbs (optional)

directions:
- Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium-high heat.
- Add the onions and cook until softened, about 6 to 8 minutes.
- Add pepper flakes and oregano, cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
- Add flour, cook for 30 seconds, then add tomatoes with juice.
- Stir in milk or cream and bring to a boil, then simmer for 3 minutes.
- Puree the soup using an immersion blender or a standing blender.
- Season with salt and pepper, stir in whiskey if using, and simmer.
- Serve with a drizzle of olive oil, herbs, and grilled cheese sandwiches.

---

These recipes provide a quick and comforting meal, with a creamy tomato soup paired perfectly with cheesy grilled sandwiches. Enjoy as a light lunch or dinner option.
