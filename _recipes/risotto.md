title:  "Basic Almost-No-Stir Risotto"
image: risotto.jpg

tags:
- rice

- creamy

components:
- 325 g risotto-style rice (such as Carnaroli, Arborio, Vialone Nano, or Bomba)
- 960 ml homemade or low-sodium canned chicken stock
- 28 g unsalted butter, plus more for finishing if desired
- 30 ml extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 medium cloves garlic, minced or grated (about 2 teaspoons)
- 2 small shallots, finely minced (about 2 tablespoons)
- 240 ml dry white wine (optional, can be replaced with extra broth)
- 180 ml heavy cream, whipped to stiff peaks
- 85 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated (about 1.5 cups)
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Chopped fresh herbs or other garnishes as desired

directions:
- Combine rice and chicken stock in a large bowl. Stir to release starch, then drain through a fine-mesh strainer for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally. Reserve the stock.
- Heat butter and oil in a skillet over medium-high heat until foaming subsides. Add rice and cook, stirring frequently, until liquid evaporates and rice turns golden, about 5 minutes.
- Add garlic and shallots, cook until aromatic, about 1 minute. Add wine, if using, and cook until reduced by half, about 5 minutes.
- Stir the reserved stock and pour all but 240 ml over the rice. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook undisturbed for 10 minutes. Stir once, cover, and cook until rice is tender, about 10 more minutes.
- Add the final 240 ml of stock, increase heat, and cook, stirring constantly, until creamy.
- Off heat, fold in whipped cream and cheese. Season with salt and pepper, stir in herbs or garnishes, and serve immediately.

---

This risotto recipe provides a creamy and flavorful base that you can customize with your favorite vegetables, mushrooms, meats, or other ingredients. Enjoy it as a comforting main dish or a sophisticated side.
