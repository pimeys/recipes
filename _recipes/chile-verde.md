directions:
- Toss the pork with salt and let it sit for 1 hour.
- Roast the poblano and cubanelle peppers until charred, then steam, peel, and chop them.
- Broil the tomatillos, garlic, and jalapeños until charred, then blend with cilantro until smooth.
- Preheat the oven to 107°C. Brown the pork in a Dutch oven, then add onions and cumin.
- Add chicken stock and the pureed chile mixture, then simmer in the oven for about 3 hours until the pork shreds easily.
- Skim off any fat, adjust consistency, stir in remaining cilantro, and season to taste. Serve with desired garnishes and warm tortillas.

components:
- 1.36 kg boneless pork shoulder, cut into 2.5 cm cubes
- Kosher salt
- 5 poblano peppers
- 5 cubanelle peppers
- 900 g tomatillos (about 15 medium), husks removed, rinsed, and patted dry
- 6 cloves garlic, unpeeled
- 2 jalapeño peppers, stems removed and split lengthwise
- 44 ml vegetable oil
- 120 g fresh cilantro leaves, loosely packed
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 960 ml chicken stock (homemade or low-sodium canned)
- Garnishes as desired

---

A hearty and flavorful Mexican-inspired dish, Chile Verde features tender pork in a rich, spicy green chile sauce. Perfect for serving with warm tortillas and your choice of garnishes.
