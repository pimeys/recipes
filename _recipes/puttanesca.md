title:  "Spaghetti Puttanesca"
image: puttanesca.jpg

tags:
- pasta
- Italian
- spicy

components:
- Kosher salt
- 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil
- 28 g unsalted butter
- 1 medium onion, finely diced (about 175 g)
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 8 anchovy fillets, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 3 tablespoons capers, rinsed, patted dry, and roughly chopped
- 1/2 cup pitted olives, roughly chopped (such as Taggiasche, Kalamata, or Manzanilla)
- 1 can (800 g) whole tomatoes packed in juice, crushed by hand
- 1 can (170 g) tuna packed in olive oil, drained (optional)
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 450 g spaghetti
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
- Grated Parmigiano-Reggiano or Pecorino Romano for serving

directions:
- Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
- Heat olive oil and butter in a saucepan until butter melts and foaming subsides.
- Add onion and cook until softened, about 3 minutes.
- Add garlic, anchovies, and pepper flakes; cook until garlic is light golden, about 3 minutes.
- Add capers, olives, and tomatoes with juice; cook until sauce reduces and oil separates, about 15 minutes.
- Fold in tuna if using, and season with salt and pepper. Set aside.
- Cook spaghetti in boiling water until al dente, reserving 1 cup of cooking liquid.
- Combine pasta with sauce, adding reserved water as needed to thin.
- Serve immediately, topped with parsley and grated cheese.

---

This classic Italian pasta dish is flavorful and comforting, perfect for a cozy night in. The combination of anchovies, capers, and olives creates a rich, savory sauce that pairs beautifully with spaghetti.
