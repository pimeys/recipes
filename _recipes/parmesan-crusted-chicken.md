directions:
- Preheat the oven to 200°C with a combi steam setting. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.
- Mix breadcrumbs, parmesan, parsley, garlic, butter, and black pepper.
- Place chicken fillets on the prepared pan and top with the breadcrumb mixture, pressing down gently.
- Bake in the preheated oven until the chicken is cooked through and the crumbs are golden, about 12 minutes.
- Serve with roasted vegetables or a green salad.

components:
- 180 g breadcrumbs (fresh, white or wholemeal)
- 100 g grated parmesan cheese
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped parsley or mixed herbs
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 60 g butter, melted
- Cracked black pepper
- 4 chicken breasts (200 g each), trimmed and halved to make 8 thin fillets


---

This parmesan-crusted chicken is juicy and tender with a crispy, savory topping. It's quick to prepare and perfect for a satisfying meal.
