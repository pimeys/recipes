components:
- 2.5 kg potatoes
- 75 ml wheat flour
- About 300 ml cooking water
- About 500 ml milk
- Salt
- 1-2 teaspoons syrup (optional)
- Butter (optional)

directions:
- Choose a potato variety that mashes well.
- Wash, peel, and boil the potatoes in salted water until tender. Drain the cooking water and mash the potatoes.
- Allow the mashed potatoes to cool slightly to a temperature just above hand warmth. Gradually stir in about 300 ml of warm cooking water to achieve a potato mass temperature of 55-70°C for mashing. Do not exceed 75°C, as the casserole will not mash properly.
- Add half of the flour to the mixture and stir until smooth.
- Place the potato mixture in a greased casserole dish and put it in the oven at 60°C to mash.
- After half an hour, stir in the remaining flour. Continue mashing for at least 3-4 hours. As it mashes, the texture will loosen and develop a sweet flavor.
- Add milk to the mashed potatoes to achieve a loose, porridge-like consistency.
- Season the mixture with salt and syrup if needed.
- Pour the mixture into a greased baking dish, filling it only halfway, as the casserole will bubble and rise during baking. If desired, add a couple of knobs of butter on top. If the surface browns too much, cover it.
- Bake the casserole at 150°C for about two hours.

---

This traditional mashed potato casserole is a comforting side dish with a sweet, creamy texture. It's perfect for serving alongside roasted meats or as part of a holiday meal.
