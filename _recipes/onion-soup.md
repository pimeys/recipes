title:  "Fast French Onion Soup"
image: french-onion-soup.jpg

tags:
- soup
- French

directions:
- Melt sugar in a Dutch oven until it caramelizes, then add onions and cook until coated.
- Add butter, baking powder, and salt, cooking until onions are golden and a brown coating forms.
- Deglaze with water multiple times until onions are deeply browned.
- Add sherry, chicken stock, bay leaves, and thyme. Simmer until flavors meld.
- Ladle soup into bowls, top with croutons and cheese, and broil until cheese is melted and bubbly.

components:
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 2.27 kg yellow onions (about 5 large), finely sliced
- 28 g unsalted butter
- 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
- Kosher salt
- 60 ml dry sherry
- 1.4 L homemade or low-sodium canned chicken stock
- 2 bay leaves
- 6 to 8 sprigs fresh thyme
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1 baguette, sliced and toasted
- 227 g Gruyère or Swiss cheese, grated

---

A quick and flavorful French onion soup with deeply caramelized onions and a rich, cheesy topping. Perfect for a comforting meal on a chilly day.
