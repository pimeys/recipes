title:  "Cannabis-Infused Sugar Cookies"

tags:
- dessert

components:
- 620 g flour, plus more for rolling
- 400 g sugar
- 240 g cannabutter
- 240 g butter
- 2 eggs
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 teaspoons salt

directions:
- Beat cannabutter, sugar, egg, and vanilla in a large bowl on medium speed until thoroughly combined.
- In a separate bowl, mix dry ingredients.
- Gradually add dry ingredients to the cannabutter mixture, stirring until fully incorporated.
- Cover the dough and refrigerate for at least one hour.
- Preheat the oven to 190°C.
- Roll out the dough on a generously floured surface to approximately 8 mm thick. Cut cookies and transfer to ungreased baking sheets.
- Bake for 10-12 minutes or until lightly golden.
- Remove from the oven, transfer to a cooling rack, and let cool completely before frosting.
