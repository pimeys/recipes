title:  "Basic Pan-Seared Pork Chops"

tags:

ingredients:
- 4 bone-in pork chops (about 170-227 g each), about 2.5 cm thick
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 15 ml vegetable oil
- 15 g unsalted butter
- 1 teaspoon minced fresh thyme (optional)

directions:
- Pat the pork chops dry and season with salt (omit if brined) and pepper.
- Heat the oil in a skillet over high heat until smoking. Add the chops and cook, flipping frequently, until lightly browned on both sides, about 5 minutes.
- Add the butter and thyme (if using), and continue cooking, turning frequently, until the chops are deep brown and reach an internal temperature of 57°C for medium, about 5 more minutes.
- Transfer the chops to a platter and let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

---

These pan-seared pork chops are juicy and flavorful, perfect for a quick and satisfying meal. Cooking them bone-in helps retain moisture for a tender result.
