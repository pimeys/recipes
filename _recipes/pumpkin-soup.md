title:  "Roasted Pumpkin Soup"
image: pumpkin-soup.jpg

tags:
- soup
- autumn
- vegetarian

components:
- 1.8 kg whole pumpkins and winter squash (a mix such as sugar, kabocha, delicata, and acorn)
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 medium onion, finely sliced (about 1 cup)
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon (optional)
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg (optional)
- 960 ml homemade or low-sodium canned chicken stock (plus more if necessary)
- 30 ml maple syrup

directions:
- Preheat the oven to 175°C and position the rack in the lower-middle.
- Cut the pumpkins and squash in half, remove the seeds, and place them cut side up on a foil-lined baking sheet.
- Rub with olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and roast until tender, about 1 hour. Let cool.
- Melt butter in a large pot, add onion, and cook until softened, about 4 minutes.
- Add cinnamon and nutmeg if using, and stir until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add chicken stock.
- Scoop out the roasted pumpkin flesh and add it to the pot with enough water to barely cover it. Simmer.
- Blend the mixture in batches until smooth, adding more stock or water if needed.
- Strain the mixture into a clean pot, reheat gently, and stir in maple syrup.
- Season with salt and pepper to taste and serve.

---

This roasted pumpkin soup is a delightful autumn dish, perfect for warming up on a chilly day. The combination of roasted pumpkin and spices creates a rich and comforting flavor.
