directions:
- Blend chicken livers, anchovies, Marmite, and soy sauce until smooth. Mix cream, stock, and gelatin; set aside.
- Heat oil, sauté garlic, oregano, and pepper flakes. Add tomatoes and reduce the sauce.
- Cook pancetta, then sauté onions, carrots, and celery.
- Brown ground meats, add anchovy mixture, and simmer with wine until reduced.
- Blend tomato sauce until smooth, then combine with milk mixture, bay leaves, and parsley. Simmer until rich and thick.
- Stir in fish sauce and Parmigiano-Reggiano until emulsified. Season and cool.

components:
- 113 g chicken livers
- 4 anchovy fillets
- 1 teaspoon Marmite
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 480 ml whole milk
- 120 ml heavy cream
- 480 ml chicken stock (homemade or low-sodium canned)
- 1 packet (7 g) powdered gelatin
- 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 medium cloves garlic, minced or grated (about 4 teaspoons)
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 can (794 g) whole tomatoes, crushed
- 113 g pancetta, cut into chunks
- 1 large onion, finely chopped (about 1.5 cups)
- 2 carrots, peeled and diced (about 1 cup)
- 3 stalks celery, diced (about 1 cup)
- 57 g unsalted butter
- 454 g ground lamb (or 85/15 ground beef)
- 454 g ground pork
- 454 g ground veal
- 14 g fresh sage leaves, finely chopped
- 240 ml red wine
- 2 bay leaves
- 1/4 cup minced fresh parsley
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce
- 113 g Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated

---

A rich and flavorful Bolognese sauce that combines a variety of meats and aromatics for a deep, complex flavor. Perfect for lasagna or serving over pasta.
