title:  "Real Texas Chili Con Carne"

tags:

directions:
- Season half the beef with salt and pepper, then brown in a Dutch oven. Set aside with the remaining meat.
- Cook onion until softened, then add garlic, spices, and oregano.
- Add all the meat, chile paste, and chicken stock. Simmer until meat is tender.
- Season with salt and pepper, thicken with cornmeal, and serve with desired garnishes.

components:
- 1.8 kg boneless beef chuck, trimmed and cut into chunks
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 30 ml vegetable oil
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced (about 4 teaspoons)
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon (optional)
- 3/4 teaspoon ground allspice (optional)
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 240 ml Chile Paste
- 1.9 L homemade or low-sodium canned chicken stock
- 2 to 3 tablespoons instant cornmeal
- Garnishes as desired

---

A hearty and flavorful Texas-style chili with tender beef and a rich, spicy sauce. Serve with your choice of toppings and enjoy with corn chips or tortillas.
