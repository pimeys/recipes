title:  "Easy Ham, Bean, and Kale Stew"
image: ham-bean-kale-stew.jpg

tags:
- stew
- comfort food

directions:
- Soak beans in salted water overnight.
- Combine beans, ham, stock, onion, garlic, bay leaves, and salt in a Dutch oven. Simmer until beans are tender.
- Remove and shred ham, discard bones, onion, garlic, and bay leaves.
- Add kale to the pot and simmer until the stew thickens and kale is tender.
- Season with salt and pepper, then serve drizzled with olive oil and sherry vinegar.

components:
- 454 g dried white beans (e.g., great northern, cannellini, or Tarbais), soaked and drained
- 454 g leftover smoked ham bones, scraps, and/or meat
- 2.8 L homemade or low-sodium canned chicken stock, or water
- 1 large onion, halved
- 1 large clove garlic
- 3 bay leaves
- Kosher salt
- 1 large bunch kale, stems trimmed (about 2 quarts loosely packed)
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Sherry vinegar

---

A hearty and comforting stew featuring tender beans, smoky ham, and nutritious kale. Perfect for a cozy meal, served with crusty bread on the side.
