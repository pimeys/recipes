title:  "Extra-Cheesy Grilled Cheese Sandwiches"
image: grilled-cheese-sandwich.jpg

tags:
- side dish
- sandwich
- cheesy

components:
- 28 g unsalted butter
- 4 slices high-quality white, whole wheat, or rye sandwich bread
- 113 g sliced Cheddar cheese
- Kosher salt
- 14 g grated Parmesan cheese (optional)
- Brown mustard

directions:
- Melt 1/2 tablespoon of butter in a skillet over medium heat.
- Toast 2 bread slices until lightly browned, top with sliced cheese, and form sandwiches.
- Melt another 1/2 tablespoon butter, sprinkle with salt, and toast sandwiches until golden brown.
- Optionally, coat sandwiches with grated Parmesan cheese and toast until melted and golden.
- Slice sandwiches diagonally and serve with mustard and tomato soup.

---

These recipes provide a quick and comforting meal, with a creamy tomato soup paired perfectly with cheesy grilled sandwiches. Enjoy as a light lunch or dinner option.
