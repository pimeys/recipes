directions:
- Preheat the oven to 220°C fan.
- Prepare the aubergines by shaving off long alternating strips of peel, slicing them into 1 cm thick rounds, and mixing with oil, salt, and pepper. Roast for 30-35 minutes until dark golden-brown.
- Sauté garlic and chillies in oil until golden-brown, then add tomatoes, oregano, sugar, salt, and pepper. Cook for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens. Remove oregano sprigs and stir in the roasted aubergine.
- Cook spaghetti until al dente, then mix with the sauce, adding most of the pecorino, basil, and some cooking water if needed.
- Serve the spaghetti in bowls, topped with the remaining pecorino, basil, and a drizzle of oil.

components:
- 3 aubergines (900g)
- 120 ml olive oil
- 5 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1-2 mild dried chillies (deseeded if you don't want the heat)
- 2 x 400g tins of plum tomatoes
- 5 large oregano sprigs (8g)
- 1 tsp caster sugar
- 300g spaghetti
- 45g mature pecorino romano (or ricotta salata), shaved
- 20g basil leaves, torn
- Salt and black pepper

---

A comforting and flavorful Italian dish, Pasta alla Norma is perfect for a satisfying meal. The combination of roasted aubergine, rich tomato sauce, and fragrant basil creates a delightful harmony of flavors.
