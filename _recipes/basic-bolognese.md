directions:
- Sprinkle gelatin over stock and set aside.
- Melt butter in a Dutch oven, sauté carrots, celery, and onions until translucent.
- Cook half of the ground meat until browned, then add the remaining meat and cook thoroughly.
- Add tomato paste, cook briefly, then deglaze with wine and add bay leaves.
- Add the stock with gelatin, season, and simmer gently for about 3 hours until thickened.
- Skim fat, discard bay leaves, stir in cream, and season to taste.

components:
- 2 packets unflavored powdered gelatin (15 g)
- 475 ml homemade or low-sodium broth
- 45 g unsalted butter
- 2 large carrots (about 375 g), peeled and finely minced
- 3 medium celery ribs (about 240 g), finely minced
- 2 medium yellow onions (about 480 g), finely minced
- 1.8 kg ground meat (beef, or a mix of beef, pork, and veal)
- 60 ml tomato paste
- 350 ml dry white or red wine
- 2 bay leaves
- Kosher salt
- Pinch freshly grated nutmeg
- 3 ml Asian fish sauce
- 120 ml heavy cream

---

A classic Bolognese sauce that's rich and flavorful, perfect for pasta dishes or lasagna. This recipe uses a blend of meats for depth of flavor and a creamy finish.
