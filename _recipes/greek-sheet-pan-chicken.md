title:  "Greek Sheet Pan Chicken"
image: greek-sheet-pan-chicken.jpg

tags:
- main course
- Mediterranean

components:
- 650 g boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 45 ml extra virgin olive oil, divided
- 15 ml balsamic vinegar
- 2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 scallions, finely sliced
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 0.5 teaspoon kosher or flaky salt
- 0.5 teaspoon black pepper
- 450 g potatoes, cut into 2.5 cm chunks
- 2 bell peppers, roughly diced
- 1 red chile pepper, sliced
- 90 g kalamata olives
- 240 g feta cheese, roughly crumbled
- 100 g unsweetened/plain Greek yogurt
- 0.5 teaspoon coarsely ground black pepper
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 0.25 teaspoon kosher or flaky salt
- 0.5 bunch fresh parsley, chopped

directions:
- Preheat the oven to 220°C with a combi steam setting. Marinate the chicken with olive oil, lemon juice, balsamic - egar, oregano, paprika, scallions, garlic, salt, and pepper. Let it rest in the fridge for 20-30 minutes.
- Toss potatoes and peppers with remaining olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and place on a baking sheet. Add -  marinated chicken on top and bake for 25-30 minutes until cooked through and golden.
- Mix chile pepper, olives, and feta for the topping. Combine yogurt, garlic, and salt for the sauce.
- Serve chicken and vegetables with the olive mixture, yogurt sauce, and garnish with parsley.

---

This flavorful Greek-inspired sheet pan chicken is easy to prepare and packed with Mediterranean flavors. Enjoy it as a hearty and satisfying meal.
