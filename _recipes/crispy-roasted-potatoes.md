directions:
- Preheat your oven to 100C, steam setting. If you don't have a steam oven, you can boil your potatoes in this step instead of steaming.
- Peel your potatoes if desired. Cut them into halves or quarters to achieve pieces roughly 4-5 cm in diameter.
- Place the potatoes in a single layer in a stainless steel pan and steam for 30 minutes until tender.
- Change the oven setting to 180C, convection setting (no steam). Drain the potatoes, shake to roughen the edges, and coat with olive oil and salt.
- Roast the potatoes for 45 minutes to an hour until golden and crispy. Avoid stirring until a good crust forms.
- Optionally, season with extra flaky salt before serving.

ingredients:
- 455 g floury potatoes
- 80 ml olive oil or duck fat
- 1.5 tsp fine salt

---

Enjoy these crispy roasted potatoes as a delightful side dish, perfectly tender inside and crispy on the outside.
